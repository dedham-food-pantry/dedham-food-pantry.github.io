Your gift helps feed more than 500 Dedham households. We're run entirely by volunteers, and the Pantry is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so donations are fully tax deductible.

<div class="ways">

<section class="way">
<h2 class="way__title">Give money</h2>

Cash donations go furthest: through the Greater Boston Food Bank, **$1 buys about $3 worth of food**.

<a class="action-btn" href="https://www.paypal.com/fundraiser/charity/106215" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Donate online<span class="visually-hidden"> with PayPal (opens in new tab)</span></a>

Or mail a check to Dedham Food Pantry, P.O. Box 46, Dedham, MA 02027. Many employers will [match your gift](/donate/donate/#double-your-donation).
</section>

<section class="way">
<h2 class="way__title">Give food and essentials</h2>

We especially need coffee, canned tuna, soups, cereal, personal care items, and gluten-free and baby foods.

**Drop-off hours:** Tuesday–Friday 10am–12pm, Saturday 12pm–2pm. For large donations or food drives, please contact us first.

</section>
