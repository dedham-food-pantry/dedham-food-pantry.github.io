Every gift helps a Dedham family put food on the table. We're a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your donation is **fully tax deductible**.

## Give Money {#donate-cash}

Cash goes furthest: we buy fresh produce, milk, eggs, meat and staples at bulk prices.

* **Mail a check** to Dedham Food Pantry, P.O. Box 46, Dedham, MA 02027
