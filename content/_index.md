## About Us

<p class="home-lead">Our mission is to <strong>end hunger in Dedham</strong>. Everyone deserves consistent access to healthy food and the dignity of support without judgment.</p>

<ul class="home-stats">
<li><span class="home-stats__number">Since 1990</span> serving our neighbors</li>
<li><span class="home-stats__number">500+</span> Dedham households</li>
<li><span class="home-stats__number">28,000</span> bags of food a year</li>
</ul>

We're here for any Dedham resident who needs help due to loss of employment, a reduction in benefits, or living on a limited income. We're non-denominational, non-political, run by volunteers, and funded entirely by [contributions][2] from our community.
