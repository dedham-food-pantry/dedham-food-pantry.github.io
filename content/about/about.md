Established in [1990][4], the Dedham Food Pantry is a cornerstone of community support with a bold mission: to end hunger in Dedham. We serve approximately 540 Dedham families (with more than 1400 family members) each month. Approximately one-third of the people that we serve are children.

We distribute a variety of non-perishable pantry staples, perishable items (such as meat, eggs, cheese and bread), and fresh seasonal produce when available. We obtain food from a variety of sources including the Greater Boston Food Bank, individual, community, and corporate food drives, warehouse and thrift stores, donated food from grocery stores, and donated produce from local farms. We also provide personal hygiene items including shampoo, soap, toothpaste, and feminine hygiene products.

We believe that everyone deserves consistent access to healthy food and the dignity of support without judgment.

The Dedham Food Pantry is non-denominational, non-political, and supported by generous [donors][1] and dedicated [volunteers][2], including a volunteer [Board of Trustees][3]. We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation so all [donations][1] are fully tax deductible. Our EIN number is 04-3098455 and we're happy to provide our current Form W-9 on request.
