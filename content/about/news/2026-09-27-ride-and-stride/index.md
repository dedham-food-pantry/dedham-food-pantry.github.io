Join the The Dedham Food Pantry’s Ride & Stride team!

We’re hitting the road (and trails!) for the 2026 Ride & Stride for Food hosted by Three Squares New England, and we’re looking for walkers, runners, and cyclists to join Team DFP!

* When: Sunday, September 27<br/>
* Where: Noble & Greenough School Dedham, MA<br/>
* How: Bike • Run • Walk

Every dollar you raise supports the Dedham Food Pantry!

Whether you’re an avid cyclist, a runner, a casual walker, or just looking for a fun way to support your neighbors, we’d love to have you on our team. Grab a friend, recruit your family, or come solo — the more Team DFP members, the more we can raise for the pantry!

Join or donate to support the Dedham Food Pantry team here: https://ride.threesquaresne.org/team/828831
