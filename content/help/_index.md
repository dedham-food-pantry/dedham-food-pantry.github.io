We provide groceries **at no cost** to Dedham residents who meet our income guidelines. Getting started takes three steps.

<ol class="steps">
<li class="step">
<h2 class="step__title">Register at the Pantry (one time only)</h2>

Visit us at [600 Washington Street](https://maps.app.goo.gl/TsfNto11XW5p3oYS6) **Tuesday–Friday, 10am–noon**. Bring:

* **Photo ID**, such as a driver's license, state ID or passport
* **Proof of your Dedham address**, such as a lease, utility bill or bank statement

You can fill out the registration form at the Pantry, or print it ahead of time: [English](/help/we-can-help/Registration-Form-ENGLISH.pdf) · [Español](/help/we-can-help/Registration-Form-ESPAÑOL.pdf) · [Kreyòl ayisyen](/help/we-can-help/Registration-Form-KREYÒL-AYISYEN.pdf)

<a class="action-btn" href="/help/we-can-help/">Who qualifies and what to bring</a>
</li>

<li class="step">
<h2 class="step__title">Reserve a time to shop</h2>

We're open for shopping:

* **Every Saturday morning**
* **First and third Wednesday evenings** of the month
* **Second and fourth Wednesday afternoons** of the month, for seniors

Reservations are recommended. Walk-ins are welcome but will shop after people with reservations.

<a class="action-btn" href="/help/signup/">Shopping times and how to reserve</a>
</li>

<li class="step">
<h2 class="step__title">Come shop</h2>

Choose the food your household needs, including fresh produce, milk, eggs, meat and pantry staples.
</li>
</ol>

## Not a Dedham resident?

We can only serve Dedham residents, but help is close by. Call or text Project Bread's FoodSource Hotline at [1-800-645-8333](tel:+18006458333), or see our [list of nearby food pantries](/help/nearby-pantries/).

## Questions?

Call or text [(781) 269-1541](tel:+17812691541) or email [manager@dedhamfoodpantry.org](mailto:manager@dedhamfoodpantry.org).
